🌎 CULTURA · EL FLAMENCO
El cante, el baile, la guitarra: un arte, tres partes

Marta's espectáculo belongs to a tradition born in Andalucía, shaped over centuries by Romani, Andalusian, and North African communities living side by side. El flamenco is not one skill but three, fused into a single performance: el cante (the raw, often improvised singing), el baile (the percussive footwork and hand-turns), and la guitarra (the rhythm that ties the other two together). No single element carries the art alone — UNESCO recognized flamenco as Intangible Cultural Heritage in 2010 precisely because that fusion, not any one part, is what makes it flamenco. It's the same idea you practiced today: separate pieces — regular verbs, spelling-change verbs, connectors — that only become a real story once they work together.
