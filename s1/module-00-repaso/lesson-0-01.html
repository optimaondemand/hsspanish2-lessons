★ ¡Bienvenidos a Español 2!
Lesson · about 40 minutes
📖 TODAY'S FOCUS
You already speak more Spanish than you think — today we switch it back on.
Welcome back. This first module is a repaso (review): we reactivate everything you built in Spanish 1 before adding anything new. We start where every conversation starts — greeting people and introducing yourself. By the end of this lesson you'll be greeting, saying where you're from, and meeting someone new, entirely in Spanish.
🎯 BY THE END OF THIS LESSON YOU WILL BE ABLE TO…
1
I will be able to greet someone appropriately for the time of day and say goodbye.
2
I will be able to introduce myself and ask & answer ¿cómo estás? and ¿de dónde eres?
3
I will be able to describe how far the Spanish-speaking world reaches.
⏰ PART 1 · PARA EMPEZAR 7 min

Switch your ear back on

Over the summer your Spanish didn't disappear — it just went quiet. The fastest way to wake it up is to use it. Read this aloud: —Hola, buenos días. Me llamo… ¿Cómo estás? Notice how much you still understand without translating.

💭 QUICK CHECK-IN · no wrong answer
It's 8:00 in the morning and you see your teacher. What do you say?
📚 PART 2 · SALUDOS Y PRESENTACIONES 11 min

Listen, then say each one back

Click the 🔊 and repeat aloud in a clear voice. These are the building blocks of every first conversation.

  • holahi / hello
  • buenos díasgood morning
  • buenas tardesgood afternoon
  • buenas nochesgood evening / good night
  • ¿cómo estás?how are you? (informal)
  • ¿cómo te llamas?what's your name?
  • me llamo…my name is…
  • mucho gustonice to meet you
  • ¿de dónde eres?where are you from?
  • soy de…I'm from…
  • adiósgoodbye
  • hasta luegosee you later
⭐ PARA IR MÁS LEJOS · optional · +5 min
Say the three time-of-day greetings in order — buenos días (morning) → buenas tardes (afternoon) → buenas noches (night). Notice días is the only one with buenos; the other two use buenas.
🧠 PART 3 · SER — QUIÉN ERES Y DE DÓNDE 8 min

The verb behind “I am” and “I'm from”

To introduce yourself you lean on ser — the verb of identity and origin. You met it in Spanish 1; here it is again, warm and ready:

SubjectserExample
yosoyYo soy Ana. / Soy de Miami.
túeres¿De dónde eres?
usted / él / ellaesElla es de México.
nosotros/assomosNosotros somos estudiantes.
ustedes / ellos / ellassonEllos son de Colombia.

To say where you're from: soy de + place. Soy de Orlando. Ella es de Guatemala. That's all you need to answer ¿de dónde eres?

Hola, buenos días. Me llamo Daniel y soy de Tampa. Mucho gusto. Yours will use your own name and city — the frame is Me llamo… y soy de…
📖 PART 5 · A LEER 5 min

—Igualmente. ¿De dónde eres, Marcos?

⭐ COMPRUEBA · optional
Where is each person from? Notice they come from two different continents — and speak the same language. That's the reach you'll see in the culture note below.
🌎 CULTURA · EL MUNDO HISPANO
Un idioma, veintiún países

Spanish is an official language in 21 countries across the Americas, Europe, and Africa, and the mother tongue of roughly 500 million people — second only to Mandarin among native speakers. The United States alone has more than 40 million Spanish speakers. When Lucía (España) and Marcos (Puerto Rico) understand each other instantly, they're sharing a language that crossed an ocean 500 years ago and grew new roots everywhere it landed. This year you'll visit a different corner of that world in every module. (In the full course this pairs with a short map activity — the Mapa del mundo hispano.)

★ LA VIRTUD · LAS SIETE VIRTUDES

This year's character thread is las siete virtudes — valor, cortesía, honestidad, perseverancia, autogobierno, servicio, and responsabilidad — seven habits Optima students practice all year, in every subject. They start today: greeting a classmate warmly, using someone's name, and stepping back into a routine after the summer are small acts of cortesía and autogobierno. Watch for one of the seven named in each lesson this module — they'll keep coming back all semester.
