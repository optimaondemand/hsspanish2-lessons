📖 TODAY'S FOCUS
The first thing your phone told you today was the time — now let's say it in Spanish.
Welcome to Module 2: Mi horario y la hora — my schedule and the time. In Module 1 you named the celular and the aplicación you check all day long. What’s the very first thing that screen shows you? La hora — the time. This module turns those devices into a way to organize your day: waking, classes, meals, sleep. Today has one job — vocab set A: telling time — and we build it entirely on Spanish you already own. Telling time uses ser (es la una… son las dos…), the identity verb you’ve had since Spanish 1. No new grammar today; the new stem-changing verbs of this module arrive later in the week, once the clock feels automatic.

⏰ PART 1 · PARA EMPEZAR 7 min

From the screen in your hand to the shape of your day

Last module you named your celular and the aplicaciones on its pantalla. Almost every one of them runs on a clock: an alarm at 7:00, a class notification, a reminder to charge the phone at night. So the natural next step is to tell the time in Spanish. Read this aloud and notice how much you already recognize: Son las siete de la mañana. Es hora de la clase de español. That little word son is just ser — the same verb behind soy and eres.

📚 PART 2 · VOCABULARIO A · DECIR LA HORA 12 min

Listen, then say each one back

Click the 🔊 and repeat aloud in a clear voice. These are the building blocks for telling any time. The first is the question; the next two are the two ways to start an answer; then come the pieces that add minutes and mark the part of the day.

  • es la unait’s one o’clock
  • son las dosit’s two o’clock

  • y mediahalf past (:30)
  • menos cuartoquarter to (:45)

⭐ PARA IR MÁS LEJOS · optional · +5 min
Two quick patterns worth noticing now. First, cuarto literally means “a quarter” and media means “half” — the same idea as English, just added on with y (and) or subtracted with menos (minus). Second, mediodía and medianoche both start with medi- (“middle”): the middle of the día and the middle of the noche. Say them as a pair.
🧠 PART 3 · REPASO · SER PARA LA HORA 8 min

One verb you already know, one small choice

Telling time is not new grammar — it’s the verb ser from Spanish 1, used in just two forms. The only decision you make is singular vs. plural, and it depends on the number of hours:

When the hour is…useExample
1:00 (one) — and mediodía / medianocheEs la / EsEs la una. · Es mediodía. · Es medianoche.
2:00 and every other hour (plural)Son lasSon las dos. · Son las diez.
add minutes past the hour+ ySon las tres y media. · Es la una y cuarto.
subtract minutes before the next hour+ menosSon las siete menos cuarto. (6:45)
mark the part of the day+ de la…Son las nueve de la noche.

Why la una but las dos? Because hora (hour) is feminine: one hour is la una, and two-or-more hours are las. That’s the whole rule — es la una is the only singular, everything else is son las…

📖 PART 5 · A LEER 5 min

«Me llamo Dania y vivo en San Salvador. Mi día empieza temprano. Son las seis de la mañana cuando suena la alarma en mi celular. La primera clase es a las siete en punto. Al mediodía almuerzo con mi familia — es la comida más importante del día. Por la tarde, a la una y media, regreso a clase. Termino a las tres menos cuarto. Por la noche, casi a la medianoche, cargo el teléfono y descanso.»

⭐ COMPRUEBA · optional
Find every time in Dania’s day. Which one uses es la (singular) instead of son las, and why? Notice how de la mañana / de la tarde / de la noche tells you which half of the day she means — the same job a.m. and p.m. do in English.
🌎 CULTURA · HONDURAS Y EL SALVADOR
El reloj del día centroamericano

In Honduras and El Salvador the day is anchored to el mediodía. Lunch — el almuerzo — is the largest meal and often a real pause: many families and businesses slow down in the early afternoon before the day picks up again. Official schedules there — buses, government offices, TV listings — usually run on the 24-hour clock, so a departure at 2:30 p.m. appears as las 14:30, even though people say las dos y media de la tarde out loud. And you can taste the schedule too: in El Salvador, pupuserías fill up at both mediodía and again de la noche, when the thick corn pupusas are the classic dinner. (In the full course this pairs with a short look at a real Central American bus schedule.)

★ LA VIRTUD · COURAGE

Archbishop Óscar Romero (El Salvador, 1917–1980) defended the poor and oppressed of San Salvador from one fixed hour each week: his Sunday radio homily, broadcast even after threats made it dangerous to speak. He was assassinated in 1980 while saying Mass, and the Church declared him a saint in 2018. Courage, like the hours you are learning to tell this week, often means simply showing up at the appointed time to say what is right.
