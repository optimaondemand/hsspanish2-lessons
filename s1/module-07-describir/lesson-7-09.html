⏰ PART 1 · PARA EMPEZAR 6 min

What's actually happening right now?

It's easy to assume two neighboring countries must be similar — same continent, same language. But courtesy (la cortesía) means slowing down and noticing the specific, present-tense details that make each place its own — paying a country the respect of actually knowing it. That's exactly what the present progressive is built for: describing what is actually happening right now, not a generalization. Look around you this second — not what's usually true, but what's true at this moment.

📖 PART 4 · A LEER 10 min

Dos vecinos, dos historias

Uruguay y Paraguay son vecinos de Argentina y Brasil, pero no son iguales. Uruguay es pequeño y tiene costa en el océano Atlántico; Paraguay es más grande que Uruguay, pero no tiene costa — está en el centro del continente.

En este momento, en Montevideo, mucha gente está tomando mate en el parque, tranquilamente. En Asunción, muchas familias están hablando guaraní en casa — en Paraguay, el guaraní y el español son bilingües desde la constitución. En la frontera con Brasil, la represa de Itaipú está generando enorme cantidad de energía — es una de las más grandes del mundo.

Uruguay es tan conocido por el fútbol como por su vida tranquila; en el campo, muchos agricultores están exportando carne y lana ahora mismo. Un estudiante paraguayo está escribiendo en guaraní y español en la misma frase, sin pensarlo — para él, es normal. Si solo miras el mapa, los dos países parecen similares. Si prestas atención, verás que cada uno tiene su propio ritmo.

🌎 CULTURA · URUGUAY Y PARAGUAY
Vecinos, no gemelos

Uruguay, one of South America's smallest countries, is known for a famously calm, secular civic culture (free public education dates to the 1870s), a deep mate-drinking ritual visible on nearly every street corner, and hosting — and winning — the very first FIFA World Cup in 1930, in Montevideo. Paraguay, landlocked at the center of the continent, is officially bilingual: Spanish and guaraní, an indigenous language still spoken at home by most of the population, appear together on money and government documents. On the border with Brazil sits the Itaipú Dam, one of the largest hydroelectric plants on Earth, jointly operated by both countries. Paraguay is also known for ñanduti, an intricate handmade lace. Same continent, same official language on paper — but look closely and Uruguay's Atlantic calm and Paraguay's bilingual, river-bound interior are two very different rhythms of life. (In the full course this pairs with a short audio clip — El mate en Montevideo y el guaraní en Asunción.)

★ LA VIRTUD · COURTESY

José Mujica (Uruguay, 1935–2025) spoke about Uruguay's neighbors, including Paraguay, with the same courteous respect he showed individuals — refusing easy stereotypes about any country or people. Today's reading asks the same of you: as you learn about Paraguay, resist flattening it into “basically like Uruguay” or reducing it to a single fact. Courtesy toward a country means the same thing courtesy toward a person means — paying it the respect of being seen accurately, on its own terms.
